About the College

Raja Lakhamagouda Science Institute, commonly known as RLSI, is a science institution in Belagavi managed by the Karnataka Lingayat Education (K.L.E.) Society.

The institute provides undergraduate and postgraduate education in science and technology-related disciplines.
